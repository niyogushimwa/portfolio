## 12. Weekly routine (10 minutes)

- **Monday:** comment thoughtfully on 2–3 posts in web development or design.
- **Wednesday:** share one small thing you learned or built (a screenshot is enough).
- **Friday:** connect with 5 people: classmates, developers in Rwanda, design communities.
- Keep your headline and portfolio link current.

---

## Common mistakes to avoid

- Do not use "Student at University of Rwanda" alone as a headline — it is not searchable.
- Do not list skills you cannot discuss in an interview.
- Do not use a photo with a busy background; your portrait already works well.
- Do not leave the About section empty or copy a generic template.
- Do not spam groups with your link. Share work and help others instead.
- Do not use different name spellings across GitHub, LinkedIn, your CV and your portfolio.
