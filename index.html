01 — About

A developer in progress, with a designer's eye.

I am building my career at the intersection of software, visual design and language. I learn by creating real projects, asking questions and improving one version at a time.

I am currently pursuing a Bachelor of Business Information Technology (BIT) at the University of Rwanda, after completing an A2 certificate in Software Development at APEKI Tumba TSS.

My practical experience comes from personal and academic projects: building small websites with HTML, CSS and JavaScript, practising C++ and Python, and exploring how modern AI tools can support learning and productivity.

Alongside code, I work with Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator and InDesign, and with computer-assisted translation tools such as OmegaT, Lara Translate and SYSTRAN. That mix helps me think about both how a product works and how it feels to use.
